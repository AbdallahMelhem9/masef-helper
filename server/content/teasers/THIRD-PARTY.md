# Third-party sources of the interview puzzles

## QuantQA dataset (IDs `Q-xxx` in `refs.json`)
Questions adapted from the QuantQA dataset by ReinforceNow, in collaboration with CoachQuant:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/ReinforceNow/quantqa — released under the MIT License.
Statements were restated, answers verified (and corrected where wrong), and all hints,
explanations, solutions and illustrations were written for this app.

## Web pages (IDs `W-xxx`)
Questions reported on Wall Street Oasis, EverythingQuant and Quantt (the page of each is
linked in the puzzle's sources). Only the questions are used, restated in our own words; the
solutions are our own.

## Books (IDs `G…`, `R…`, `H…`)
Zhou, *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews*; Joshi, Denson & Downes,
*Quant Job Interview Questions and Answers*; Crack, *Heard on the Street*. Questions restated,
with page references; hints, explanations, solutions and illustrations are our own.
